Our story

Founded in 1996, Infina Finance Private Limited is a Non-Banking Financial Company, registered with the Reserve Bank of India, catering to various financial needs.

Infina is majorly owned by Kotak Family. Kotak Mahindra Bank continues to hold 19% through its subsidiary. Infina offers services like Lending Against Securities, Lending Against Property, Commercial Real Estate & Insurance as a Corporate Agent.
